Your prompts, in every AI. Three things to know, then try it right here.

1

Look for the pill

Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot. Reload any tab that was already open. This appears bottom-right:

P Save   Improve
2

Type, then press Improve

Type something rough in the chat box, e.g. write an email to my landlord about the broken heater. Click Improve on the pill (or press Alt+I). Check the result, press Use this, then Enter.

Prompt or Brief? Prompt is for a small job or one you have already started: one rough line, tidied up. Brief is for a bigger job: flip the switch at the top of Improve, fill in the job, why, rules, done looks like, and you get a long, detailed prompt.

3

Keep the good ones

Press Alt+S (or Save on the pill) to keep what you typed. Click the PromptDrawer icon in your toolbar to open the drawer and Insert any saved prompt into any chat.

No AI tab needed. Type a rough request and press Improve. The result follows CIDI: Context, Instructions, Details, Input.

Honest note about the engines

Quick tidy-up is not AI. It wraps your words in a structure (role, task, requirements, format) that AI assistants respond well to. It will never rewrite your request.

Ask the AI in this chat (free) hands the rewriting to ChatGPT, Claude or whichever AI you are talking to: press Use this, then Enter, and copy the prompt it writes back.

Chrome's built-in AI and your own API key do real AI rewriting without leaving the dialog. Set them up under Settings.

Everything is stored on this device. No account, no server, no analytics. Shortcuts can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
