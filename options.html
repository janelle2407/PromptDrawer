PromptDrawer settings

Free

Everything here is stored in this browser profile. PromptDrawer has no server and no analytics.

Shortcuts

Inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot: Alt+S saves what you are typing, Alt+I improves it, Alt+D opens the drawer. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

About me

A few lines the AI should know about you (who you are, how you like answers). Use {{about_me}} in any prompt to drop it in.

Projects

A project is a reusable brief (context + instructions) you pick at the top of the drawer. Improve and Alt+I add it automatically; Brief the AI puts it into a new chat; {{project}} drops it into any saved prompt.

Improve engine

How Improve rewrites a rough request. You can switch engines in the Improve dialog too.

Your key stays on this device and is only ever sent to the provider you choose. Saving asks Chrome for permission to contact that provider.
