PromptDrawer

Free

Type a rough request. PromptDrawer turns it into a CIDI prompt (Context, Instructions, Details, Input) the AI can act on well.

  1. Open an AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot). Reload the tab if it was already open. A small P Save · Improve pill appears bottom-right.
  2. Type something rough in the chat box. Then press Alt+I or click Improve on the pill. Check the result, press Use this.
  3. Prompt or Brief? A prompt is for a small job or one you have already started: one rough line, tidied up. A brief is for a bigger job: flip the switch at the top of Improve, fill in the job, why, rules and what done looks like, and you get a long, detailed prompt. Only the job is required.
  4. Keep the good ones: press Alt+S to save what you typed. Find it later under My prompts and press Insert.
  5. No AI tab open? Use the Improve tab here in the drawer, then Copy. Two chat boxes on one page? Click in the one you want first; PromptDrawer writes into the box you used last.
  6. Same background every time? Make a Project (top of the drawer): who it is for, what it is, how the AI should work. Pick it and every Improve knows the background. Brief the AI drops the whole thing into a fresh chat; {{project}} drops it into any saved prompt.

Want real AI rewriting rather than a tidy-up? Pick "Ask the AI in this chat" (free) or add your own API key in Settings (⚙).
